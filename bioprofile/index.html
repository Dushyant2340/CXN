Privacy, sources & corrections

Privacy

Publish only information appropriate for a public-interest biography. Do not publish private contact details, precise home addresses, sensitive personal data or unverified allegations.

Sources and verification

Prefer reliable independent sources. “Sources reviewed” means an editor reviewed the citations, not that the platform guarantees every claim.

Corrections and removal

Before launch, configure a monitored contact address and a working correction/removal process. This starter does not yet provide a correction-request backend.
